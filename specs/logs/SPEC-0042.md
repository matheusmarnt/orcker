## S7 — the human ruling cannot be verified by a subagent

Round 2's second blocker is structural, not a defect in the work. The human
approval obtained on 2026-08-31 came through the interactive session. The
supervisor runs as a subagent: it sees the repository and this log, but not the
session, so an approval relayed to it is indistinguishable from an approval
invented by the cycle. It refused to ratify it, and refusing was correct - an
agent asserting that the human approved is exactly what the human-only
transition in SDD section 5 exists to prevent.

The consequence is a harness gap worth naming: with `/spec-next` requiring
`status: approved` in the committed record, a spec that reaches the loop
*without* that record can never be closed by the supervisor, no matter how the
human rules mid-cycle, because there is no artifact the human authors. Carried
to the human rather than worked around here.
