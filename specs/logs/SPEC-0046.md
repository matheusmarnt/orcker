## S2 — plan

`socket_candidates()` (`bin/orcker/src/elevate.rs:499`) drops into
`user_socket_candidates(uid)` whenever `SUDO_UID` is set, which only ever
builds the two uid-derived candidates (`/run/user/$uid/orcker/orcker.sock`,
`/tmp/orcker-$uid/orcker.sock`) and never looks at `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, so
`sudo env XDG_RUNTIME_DIR=... orcker elevate` cannot reach a dev daemon
isolated per `docs/developer/building.md`'s recipe.

`user_socket_candidates` gains a second parameter, `xdg_runtime_dir: Option<&str>`,
instead of reading the env itself, so it stays pure and table-testable; only
`socket_candidates` (the sole caller) reads `std::env::var("XDG_RUNTIME_DIR")`.
When present and non-empty, `{dir}/orcker/orcker.sock` is prepended ahead of
the two existing candidates, which are otherwise untouched (R1: no weakening
of the uid-based fallback). Both existing call sites of `socket_candidates()`
(`report_browser_trust` L138, `fetch_facts` L448) already loop over every
candidate and take the first that answers, so neither needed a change.
