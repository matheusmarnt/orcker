## S7b — supervisor verdict, attempt 2: APPROVE

With one evidence correction, recorded here because S5b as written overstates
what it proves:

The wording criterion is line-based, so it cannot see text that wraps. A third
correct negation survives at `.github/instructions/orckerd.instructions.md:36-38`
where `native` and `runtime` straddle the wrap; the grep never matched it. Its
GREEN therefore proves "these literals appear on no single line", not "no stale
prose remains". R2 is complete on content — the supervisor re-verified
independently with a whole-file, whitespace-normalised sweep of every tracked
file under `.github/`, finding exactly one hit and that a correct negation — but
the criterion is weaker than S5b claims.

Do not promote this grep into `scripts/` or into a future AC. Besides being
line-based, it forbids the literal `PHP-FPM`, which `docs/PRD.md:14` makes a
*current* product term: the app container runs PHP-FPM + Supervisor. A durable
guard belongs in its own spec and must target assertions about *host-side*
runtimes, not substrings.
